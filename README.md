# SpiderEmbodiment 🕷️  
*A Virtual Reality Environment for Studying Embodiment, Presence, and Anxiety*

## Overview
SpiderEmbodiment is a Unity-based Virtual Reality research environment designed to investigate **embodiment and presence in non-human avatars** and their effects on **emotional and behavioral responses**, particularly anxiety.

Participants embody a spider avatar in immersive VR and perform structured interaction and navigation tasks. The system is intended as an **experimental platform for Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) and XR research**, with a focus on body ownership, sensorimotor coupling, and affective experience.

## Research Focus
The project is developed to support research on:

- **Embodiment & body ownership** in non-human avatars  
- **Presence** in immersive VR environments  
- **Anxiety and affective responses** during embodied interaction  
- **Behavioral markers** such as head rotation, movement patterns, and task performance  

The core research question is how **sensorimotor congruency and first-person embodiment** influence presence and emotional experience when controlling a non-human body.

## Experimental Design
- First-person spider embodiment in VR  
- Task-based interaction and navigation scenarios  
- Controlled environmental conditions (e.g., mirror feedback, washout scenes)  
- Logging of behavioral and interaction data for later analysis  

The system is designed to be modular and extensible, allowing future integration of additional measures (e.g., physiological sensors).

## Technical Stack
- **Unity** (XR pipeline)  
- VR interaction via head and controller input  
- Custom scripts for movement, embodiment logic, and data logging
